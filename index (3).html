Corporate Internship Project · 2026

Corporate governance quality & financial performance

A five-year analysis of Coal India Limited, covering FY2020–21 to FY2024–25.

GQI / FINANCIAL SIGNALS
Coal India LimitedCompany Secretarial DepartmentApril – July 2026Research · Excel · Ratios

Research question

How did corporate governance quality and financial performance move together at Coal India Limited across five financial years?

Approach

I reviewed annual reports and Corporate Governance Reports, scored seven governance dimensions, calculated profitability and return ratios, and decomposed ROE using a three-stage DuPont framework.

My contribution

  • Constructed a seven-dimension Governance Quality Index (105-point scale).
  • Calculated Net Profit Margin, ROCE, EBITDA Margin and ROE in Excel.
  • Used three-stage DuPont analysis to examine margin, asset turnover and leverage.
  • Calculated Pearson correlations and reported them as descriptive associations.
  • Designed the Governance-Performance Analytics System (GPAS) concept and demonstrated a prototype to Company Secretarial leadership (15 June 2026).
Scope matters: This is a single-company study with five annual observations (n = 5). The patterns are descriptive; correlation does not establish causation. Because the sample is so small, VAR modelling and Granger causality tests were deliberately not applied. GPAS was a demonstration-stage prototype (Level 1) and is not a deployed company system.
Academic team project

Open-ended vs. closed-ended mutual funds

Compared three-year risk-adjusted performance across eight debt and equity mutual funds, using Sharpe Ratio, standard deviation and NAV volatility to explore liquidity and stability trade-offs. Led a five-member team, allocating tasks and consolidating the analysis.

Analytics concept

Governance-Performance Analytics System

Proposed an integrated monitoring concept for governance indicators, financial measures, data-quality checks and visual reporting. The prototype used Google Sheets, NotebookLM and Google Data Studio; additional automation and organisational deployment remain proposed, not validated outcomes.

DuPont decomposition

Why ROE peaked, then eased

ROE = Net Profit Margin × Asset Turnover × Financial Leverage. Margins expanded and leverage fell, while asset turnover stayed within 0.55×–0.62×. ROE therefore peaked at 46.31% in FY2022–23 and eased to 35.68% by FY2024–25, even though margins stayed well above their FY2020–21 level.
